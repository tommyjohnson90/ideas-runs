## Pricing and go-to-market strategist

### Bottom line
No free sites to businesses before Jan 1, 2027; they come back only through the contrarian's trust-objection test. Up to 3 well-networked "founding clients" pay the normal $250 and earn it back: $125 for each of their first two referred sales, then $50 each, the same as everyone. Anyone can earn the $50 referral, and the person referring can keep it or give it to the new owner as $50 off. Up to 2 community-group sites are free (Greenfield Girls Softball League first). The price stays $250.

### Price and model for v1

**What owners compare $250 with:**
- Wix or Squarespace: about $16 to $49 a month, so $192 to $588 a year, and the owner builds the site themselves (Elementor, websitesetup.org).
- A freelancer build: $1,000 to $5,000.

No owner has objected to $250 (current-state.md s.8). The bottleneck is reaching owners and following up. About 5 or 6 owners have been pitched, and Lobanton's follow-up is overdue (contrarian, advocate).

**The v1 offer: one price and one referral rule for everyone, plus two narrow extras.**

1. **Price.** $250, flat and one time, held through the trial. Record it as a decision; it has none today. Nobody on the board argued for moving it.

2. **Referral, open to anyone.**
   - Anyone who introduces a business that buys earns $50, paid customer or not.
   - **Give-or-get:** the introducer either keeps the $50 or gives it to the new owner as $50 off, so they pay $200. The total stays $50 either way.
   - Where the introducer works for the buying business (the Executive Cuts and Impulse stylists, I-173 and I-174), the cash is paid only with the buying owner's written knowledge, or the introducer takes the "give" option instead (legal row 6).
   - The line for the counter and the leave-behind card: "$250, done, yours. Five neighbours who buy repays your site." This is the advocate's line, and it is the $50 rule restated.

3. **Founding-client earn-back** (the CFO's Option C3, which I join).
   - Up to 3 well-networked owners pay $250.
   - They get $125 back for each of their first two referred sales, so two sales make the site free.
   - After that they are on the standard $50 terms.
   - Founders are picked by the network score below. They can include owners who have already seen $250 (Madison Hart, Descavich Plumbing), because they pay the same price.

4. **Free sites go only to community groups.** At most 2 before Jan 1, with the Greenfield Girls Softball League first (10 sponsoring businesses, including Descavich). Each needs a written scope first, and no town-run committees (legal row 5).

5. **Partner conversations, $0 and no free site required.** Approach the Keene Downtown Group (its "Dig Into Keene" programme is a $499,749.73 NBRC grant for visibility during construction for 95 downtown businesses) and the Greenfield Business Association. Ask for a newsletter mention or a meeting slot. Members get the standard offer, with no member discount.

6. **Pay-on-launch option** (joins the contrarian's motion). The owner may pay once the site is live at their own address. This removes the "Venmo a stranger" step for $0 of cash risk to Tom, since he does not host.

### Market test for v1 (offer, sample, pass mark, weeks)
6 weeks, to about Jan 4, 2027. Tom has about 110 hours (context.md); planned use is under Unit economics.

- **Sample:**
  - every owner reached through Jan 1 hears the standard offer (expect about 20 to 30);
  - up to 3 founders asked in weeks 1 and 2;
  - 1 community site;
  - 2 partner approaches.
- **Pass by week 6, all of:**
  - at least 4 paid sites;
  - at least 1 founder paid at $250;
  - at least 6 named introductions logged;
  - at least 1 paid sale that came from a referral.
- **Record each give-or-get choice.** This is a cheap local check of Gershon et al.'s finding that a benefit to the friend counts for more than one to the referrer.
- **Gate for any free business site:** the contrarian's test. Trust or legitimacy must be the most common objection, logged word for word, across 20 or more owners reached. If it trips, a 2-site pilot follows, with written terms.
- **Kill:** fewer than 2 paid sites by week 6. The problem is then the offer or the way owners are reached, not referrals, and it goes back to the board.

### Changed after team review
1. **Dropped the 4 free business anchor sites. I now support 0 before Jan 1, behind the contrarian's gate.** Reasons:
   - The two-price problem inside a cluster (customer-advocate.md).
   - Every demo popup already shows "$250" to its owner (customer-experience.md).
   - Gershon et al. 2020: rewarding the sender adds little, and a benefit to the friend moves results. A free site pays the wrong half (contrarian.md).
   - Madison referred with no gift (I-165).
   - Tom's extra hours (about 110, not 55) make free sites affordable, but not worth it. The hours go further on selling, where the trial's question gets answered.
2. **Narrowed the two-tier referral ($125, $125, then $50) from every buyer to up to 3 founders, joining the CFO's earn-back.** At 50% of price on every customer's first two referrals, it put money on the referrer, which the evidence says matters least. The standard rule is now $50 with give-or-get.
3. **Added give-or-get** (customer-experience.md motion 9), sized at $50 in total. This applies Gershon's finding at no extra cost, rather than the contrarian's $25 extra for the friend. It also gives an employee who introduces their own employer a clean option (legal row 6).
4. **Changed "open the $50 to anyone" to anyone except an employee of the buying business, unless that business's owner knows in writing.** This follows legal row 6 on commercial bribery.
5. **Community sites:** at most 2 before Jan 1, GGSL first, no town committees. This moves away from my round-1 plan to fold them into a cap shared with business anchors.
6. **Hours redone at about 110.** Unit economics now use the CFO's 6.5 hours a site.
7. **Tracking:** Open Brain cannot search terms text (context.md). The searchable marker goes in the deal title, "Website (referral: <referrer>)", plus a "Referral given" interaction. I've passed this to ops's runbook motion.
