**Secretary's check for this motion (read-only, 2026-11-23): does search_interactions match on an interaction's subject text?**

*Answer: partly, by meaning only, so it cannot serve as a ledger lookup.*

- The tool's own description says it searches "by meaning". Unlike search_thoughts, it does not claim keyword matching.
- **Test 1.** The query "only barbers on" appears only in the subject of I-162 ("Walk-in at The Arcade: only barbers on, no one free to talk"). I-162 ranked first at 65.8%. I-163, whose subject says "only stylists on" and whose body is word for word the same, ranked fifth at 61.4%. So the subject does count toward the match.
- **Test 2.** The query "Touch 1 of 5" put I-179 first at 67.7%. Results 2 to 5 were unrelated: I-165, I-164, a 2021 "Phone tag" text (I-135) and I-163. Results are ranked by similarity, not filtered by the string.
- **Test 3.** The query "Referral given", with no such interactions yet, returned five loosely related referral interactions (I-54, I-56, I-55, I-136, I-173) at 68% to 71%.
- **Conclusion.** An interaction subject helps ranking. It does not give an exact or complete list, and results are capped at 25 per call. The reliable exact lookup is the **deal title**: list_opportunities `query` matches "part of the deal title ... any case" (its schema). The title marker in (a) should therefore be the index, and interactions the detail read through account_brief or get_opportunity. Applies only if M1.7 passes.
