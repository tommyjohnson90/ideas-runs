## Item 1: free sites for well-networked owners

**Question (Tom's agenda):** "Should I give away a dozen or so free sites to business owners that likely have a large network, with the expectation that they would refer me to their friends for a quality, inexpensive site?" and, if yes, who to pick, what the site includes, what to ask in return, how referrals are tracked and rewarded, and how this fits the $250 price and the $50 referral.

**Discussion.** No member supported the dozen; the CFO put it at about 75 to 80 hours, about 70% of the remaining trial (guess). The board split 3 to 3 on whether to give any free business sites in the trial: the contrarian, pricing and the customer advocate for none (version A); the CFO, ops and customer experience for up to 4, given 2 then 2 behind a gate (version B). The synthesizer recommended A on the evidence: 85 demo popups and two direct quotes already say $250, so a free site in the same clusters creates a second price; no trust objection has been logged; the one real referral (I-165) came without a gift. It noted that version B had lost its original proposer, pricing having withdrawn its cap of 4 in round 2. The board agreed instead on making "free" something any owner can earn through referrals, on up to 2 community-group sites, and on written terms and tracking before any payout. Every hour figure in the record is a member's guess: no site has yet been delivered and timed.
